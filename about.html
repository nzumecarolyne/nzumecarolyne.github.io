Life Story

🌸

Celebrating the journey of Carolyne Nzume

🌸

Mrs. Nzume née Carolyne Bessem Lobe, a beacon of peace, love, kindness, gentleness and compassion. She was born on the 12th April 1961 in Kembong, Manyu Division to Pa Netongo Lobe (of blessed memory) and Ma Esther Lobe nee Ashu Mbu.

Mrs. Nzume lived a remarkable life filled with passions and accomplishments.

Early Life & Education
Academic Achievements

Academic Life

With regards to her academic life, she attended several Presbyterian, Catholic and Government primary and secondary schools across Kumba, Mamfe, Besongabang and obtained the GCE A' levels at CCAST Bambili in 1983.

She proceeded to the University of Yaounde for two years and gained admission into ENAM, where she trained as a Labour Controller.

Professional Career
Service to the Nation

Professional Life

Concerning her professional life, she worked for the Ministry of Labour and Social Security in various capacities and regions, serving as Service Head for the Regional Delegations in both the North (Garoua) and South West (Buea) Regions.

She was also the Divisional Delegate for Fako for a few years before returning to Garoua, where she retired as Labour Administrator in 2016.

A tribute to Carolyne Nzume – Celebrating a life of service and love

Family Life

As far as family life is concerned, Mrs. Nzume got married to her soul mate and devoted husband, Colonel Kenneth Akwe Nzume on the 26th August 1986. They were blessed with both biological and adopted children, most of whom are now successful and responsible adults as a result of the sacrifices and parental guidance.

She was a devoted wife, mother, grandmother, daughter, sister and friend, whose warmth and generosity touched and transformed the lives of all who crossed her path. Her kindness knew no bounds, and she treated everyone indiscriminately irrespective of class, gender, status or rank. She played an active role in transforming so many lives and made them become a better version of themselves.

"She treated everyone indiscriminately irrespective of class, gender, status or rank."

— A woman of grace and equality

Family & Loved Ones
Faith & Church Service

Faith & Church Life

Beyond her family life, she was a God-fearing woman, a fervent and committed Presbyterian; a devoted CWF member and a chorister. She worshipped at P.C. Garoua for 32 years where she was an elder chorister in the English Choir and CWF President. She also worshipped at P.C. Great Soppo Buea and P.C. Bonadikombo. Despite her desire to continue with church activities, ill health restricted her active and total participation.

A Life Well Lived
Celebration of Her Life

Travels & Joys

Outside her church activities, Mrs. Nzume enjoyed spending time and celebrating with family members and friends. She loved travelling and visited several countries worldwide: China, USA, France, England, Israel, Dubai, to name just a few. She always encouraged everyone around her not to miss out on any opportunity to enjoy and have fun with the phrases:

"LES BEAU JOURS SONT RARE"

"The beautiful days are rare"

"ONE LIFE TO LIVE"

Carolyne's encouragement to seize every moment

Final Days & Legacy

Mrs. Nzume started experiencing health challenges 8 years ago. However, her strong faith in God, hope and positive outlook on life kept her strong as she repeatedly confessed that "I be Jesus ye follow back"!

Her sporadic periods of ill-health notwithstanding, she kept on loving, caring and being kind to all around her. On the 5th of July, she experienced some discomfort and was hospitalized for close to a week and discharged. At a time when we thought she was regaining her health, she passed away quietly on the 18th July 2026.

Carolyne Nzume - Celebration of Life

She leaves behind her loving husband, children, grandchildren, mother, sisters, brother, in-laws, cousins & nieces, nephews and other family members, neighbours and a host of friends who all adored her.

She will be remembered for her gentle spirit, unwavering faith and the love she poured in every relationship.

May her legacy live on!

May her gentle soul rest in perfect peace!

"Jesus ye follow back"

Fare thee well, Carolyne
