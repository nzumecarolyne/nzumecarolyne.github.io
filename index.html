A Remarkable Life of Faith, Love, Compassion, and Service

"I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith. Now there is in store for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will award to me on that day."

— 2 Timothy 4:7-8

Mrs. Nzume, née Carolyne Bessem Lobe, was a beacon of peace, love, kindness, gentleness and compassion. Born on 12 April 1961 in Kembong, Manyu Division, she lived a life that touched countless hearts and transformed many lives.

Her journey was one of unwavering faith, selfless service, and boundless love. Through her roles as a devoted wife, mother, grandmother, and servant of God, she left an indelible mark on everyone who had the privilege of knowing her.
